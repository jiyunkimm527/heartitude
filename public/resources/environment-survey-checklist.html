Purpose: This checklist helps volunteers systematically assess the physical environment of schools and hospital spaces in Panama. Data collected feeds directly into Heartitude's ongoing BioRhythm Research — identifying practical, low-cost improvements that help children focus better and recover faster.

No special equipment needed. All measurements can be taken using free smartphone apps (see note below). Complete one checklist per room or space assessed.
Recommended Apps:  ·  Noise: NIOSH SLM (iOS/Android) or Decibel X  ·  Light: Lux Light Meter (iOS/Android)  ·  Temperature: Record from any available thermometer on site
